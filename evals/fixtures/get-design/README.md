# Generic analytics dashboard

The page is for operations managers who need to identify failing jobs and retry them.

Current interface:

- A large heading says "Supercharge your workflow".
- A purple-to-blue gradient fills the background.
- A badge above the heading says "NEXT GENERATION".
- Four identical rounded cards show Users, Uptime, Speed, and Growth.
- Every feature card has an icon inside a rounded square above its heading.
- Gray helper text sits on colored card backgrounds.
- Status dots pulse even when jobs are idle.
- The primary retry action is visually equal to export and settings.
- The page has no loading, empty, error, disabled, or permission state.
- On mobile, the card row overflows horizontally and the action bar is clipped.
